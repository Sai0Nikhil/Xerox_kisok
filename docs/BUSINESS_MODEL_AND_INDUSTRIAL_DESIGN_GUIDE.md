## 3. How to Make the Kiosk Look Commercial & Ultra-Professional
*(Transitioning from a student DIY carpentry box to a ₹2,00,000+ commercial product look)*

### A. Material & Enclosure Finishing
1. **Move from MDF to 1.6mm CRCA Sheet Metal**:
   - MDF edges always give away a "carpentry project" look. 
   - Use **1.6mm Cold Rolled Close Annealed (CRCA) Mild Steel**, laser-cut and CNC-bent.
   - Apply **Fine-Texture Electrostatic Powder Coating** in **Deep Anthracite Matte (`RAL 7016`)** or **Matte British Racing Green (`#1B3B2B`)** with metallic champagne-gold accents.
2. **5mm–10mm CNC Bent Radii**:
   - Avoid sharp 90-degree boxy wooden seams. CNC sheet-metal corner radius gives an authentic ATM / airport kiosk look.

### B. Front Fascia: Full-Flush Smoked Glass / Acrylic Panel
- Do not mount the screen inside a cutout hole like a picture frame.
- Instead, place a **single continuous 3mm toughened smoked glass or black cast acrylic sheet** across the entire upper half.
- The 15.6" screen is optically bonded or flush-bracketed directly behind this single black glass panel so that when the screen is OFF, the entire upper face looks like a seamless black mirror.

### C. Concealed Fasteners & Hinges (Zero Visible Screws)
- **Zero Front Screw Heads**: Use internal weld studs or blind press-fit fasteners (`PEM nuts`) on the rear of panels.
- **Internal Piano / Pivot Hinges**: Maintenance doors should have internal concealed hinges so no hinges protrude on the outside.

### D. Architectural Lighting (Hidden Glow)
- Install a recessed **4000K warm-white / neutral-white LED strip** hidden under the sloped console lip and inside the paper retrieval slot.
- Illuminates the collection tray automatically when printing without visible LED bulbs.

### E. Professional Commercial Branding
- Never use hand-cut vinyl stickers or paper labels.
- Use **Direct Flatbed UV Printing** directly on the metal/acrylic panel, or **Laser-Cut 3D Metallic Acrylic Badges** for the `Bittu` logo.
